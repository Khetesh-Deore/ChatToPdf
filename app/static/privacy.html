Privacy

Last updated: 2024

What we process

When you submit a share link or upload an HTML file, the server fetches the conversation content solely to convert it to PDF. No conversation text, images, or metadata are stored beyond what is needed to complete your request.

Temporary files

Generated PDF files and intermediate HTML files are stored in temporary server storage and deleted automatically after 30 minutes. They are not accessible to other users.

Logs

The server logs the URL hostname and job status (success or error type) for operational monitoring. It does not log conversation content, full URLs, or personally identifying information.

Third-party services

This service does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Uploaded files

HTML files you upload are processed in memory to extract conversation content and are not persisted to disk beyond temporary processing. The resulting PDF follows the same 30-minute retention policy.

Contact

If you have questions about how your data is handled, please open an issue on the project repository.
